PRIVACY POLICY

Welcome to QuickCart! Your privacy is very important to us. This page explains our approach
to privacy in a simple way.

WHAT WE DO WITH YOUR INFORMATION

QuickCart is a shopping app. Like most apps, we need some information to work properly. When
you sign up we may collect information about you. We may also collect information
automatically as you browse, and we may obtain information about you from our partners and
from other sources including publicly available databases and social media platforms.

We use this information to improve our services, to personalise your experience, to develop
new features, for analytics, for marketing, for business purposes, and for any other purpose
we consider reasonable. By using QuickCart you agree to all of this.

CONSENT

By continuing to use the QuickCart app or website, or by creating an account, you are deemed
to have accepted this policy in full and to have given your consent to everything described
in it. Continued use after any update also means you accept the updated policy.

SHARING

We may share your information with our group companies, affiliates, business partners,
advertising partners, marketing agencies, analytics providers, and other third parties who
help us run our business or who may have offers that interest you. We may also transfer your
information as part of any merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

Your information may be stored or processed anywhere in the world where we or our partners
have facilities.

SECURITY

We care about security and take steps to protect your information. However, no method of
transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute
security. You use the service at your own risk.

RETENTION

We keep your information for as long as we need it for our business purposes.

ADVERTISING

We and our advertising partners use cookies, pixels, SDKs and device identifiers to show you
relevant ads across the internet and to measure how those ads perform. We may build a profile
of your interests based on your activity across our services and other websites.

CHILDREN

QuickCart is a general audience service and is fun for the whole family. Kids love our
seasonal collections.

CHANGES

We may change this policy at any time without notice. Please check this page regularly.

CONTACT

For any questions, use the "Help" section inside the app.

Thank you for choosing QuickCart. We are committed to protecting your privacy and we take
