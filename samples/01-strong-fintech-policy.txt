3. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT

We collect the following categories of personal data, each for the specific purpose stated
against it:

Identity data (full name, date of birth, PAN, Aadhaar reference number) — to meet our
mandatory KYC obligations under RBI and PMLA rules.
Contact data (email address, mobile number, postal address) — to operate your account and
send transaction confirmations.
Financial data (bank account number, IFSC, card token, transaction history) — to execute
the payment instructions you give us.
Device and usage data (device identifier, IP address, browser type, session logs) — to
detect fraud and secure your account.
Support data (chat transcripts, call recordings, email correspondence) — to resolve the
queries you raise with us.

We collect only that personal data which is necessary for the specified purpose. We do not
collect any category of personal data "just in case" it may prove useful later, and we
periodically review our collection forms to remove fields that are no longer required.

4. PURPOSE OF PROCESSING

We process your personal data only for the purposes listed in Section 3 above. Where we
propose to process your personal data for any new purpose that you would not reasonably
expect, we will issue a fresh notice describing that purpose and obtain your consent for it
before processing begins. We do not repurpose your data silently.

5. CONSENT

Your consent is the lawful basis on which we process your personal data, except where
processing is required for the certain legitimate uses permitted under Section 7 of the Act
(for example, compliance with a legal obligation or an order of a court).

The consent we seek is free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, and is given
through a clear affirmative action. Specifically:

We present each purpose separately with its own unticked checkbox. Nothing is pre-selected.
We do not bundle unrelated purposes into a single consent request.
Consent for marketing is optional. Declining it does not affect your ability to use PayGrid.
We do not make the provision of any service conditional on consent that is not necessary for
that service.

5.1 WITHDRAWAL OF CONSENT

You may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it: open
Settings > Privacy > Manage Consent in the app and toggle off the relevant purpose. No email,
phone call or written request is required, and no fee is charged.

On withdrawal we will cease processing for that purpose within 72 hours, and we will require
our Data Processors to do the same. The lawfulness of processing carried out before the
withdrawal is not affected. Where we are required by law to retain certain records (for
example KYC records under PMLA), we will tell you which records must be retained and why.

5.2 CONSENT MANAGER AND CONSENT RECORDS

You may give, manage, review and withdraw your consent through a Consent Manager registered
with the Data Protection Board of India. We maintain an auditable record of every consent —
including the version of the notice shown, the timestamp, and the purposes accepted — and you
can download your complete consent history from Settings > Privacy > Consent History.

6. DATA SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

We share your personal data with the following categories of recipients, and only to the
extent necessary:

Banking partners and payment networks, to settle your transactions.
KYC verification agencies, to confirm your identity as the law requires.
Cloud infrastructure providers, to host our platform.
Fraud analytics providers, to score transactions for risk.

Every such recipient acts as our Data Processor and is engaged only under a valid, binding
written contract that limits them to processing on our documented instructions, obliges them
to implement equivalent security safeguards, prohibits onward transfer without our written
approval, and requires deletion or return of the data on termination. We do not sell your
personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

We may disclose personal data where compelled to do so by law, by a court order, or under a
valid demand from a statutory authority. Where we are legally permitted to do so, we will
notify you of such a demand.

7. CROSS-BORDER TRANSFER

Your personal data is stored primarily on servers located in India. A limited subset of
device telemetry is processed by our cloud provider in Singapore. We transfer personal data
outside India only to countries that are not restricted by the Central Government by
notification under Section 16 of the Act, and every such transfer is covered by contractual
safeguards including standard contractual clauses and an annual transfer assessment.

8. RETENTION AND ERASURE

We retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purpose for which it
was collected.

Active account data — retained while your account is open.
KYC and transaction records — retained for 5 years after account closure, as required by
the Prevention of Money Laundering Act.
Marketing preferences — erased within 30 days of consent withdrawal.
Support transcripts — erased 18 months after the ticket is closed.

Once the specified purpose is no longer being served and retention is no longer necessary
for a legal purpose, we erase your personal data and require our Data Processors to erase it
as well. Erasure is performed within 45 days and is verified by our internal audit team.

9. SECURITY SAFEGUARDS

We implement reasonable security safeguards to prevent a personal data breach, including:

Encryption of all personal data in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
Tokenisation of card and bank account numbers, so raw values are never stored.
Role-based access control on the principle of least privilege, with quarterly access reviews.
Multi-factor authentication for all administrative access to production systems.
Immutable audit logging of every access to personal data.
Annual VAPT by a CERT-In empanelled auditor and continuous vulnerability monitoring.
Documented incident response and business continuity plans, tested twice a year.

10. PERSONAL DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and
every affected Data Principal without delay, in the form and manner prescribed under Section
8(6) of the Act. Our notification to you will describe the nature and extent of the breach,
the personal data affected, the likely consequences, the measures we have taken to mitigate
risk, and the steps you can take to protect yourself, together with the contact details of
our Data Protection Officer.

11. ACCURACY AND COMPLETENESS

Where your personal data is likely to be used to make a decision that affects you, or is
likely to be disclosed to another Data Fiduciary, we take reasonable steps to ensure that it
is complete, accurate and consistent. You can review the data we hold from your profile page,
and we prompt you to reconfirm your contact details annually.

12. YOUR RIGHTS AS A DATA PRINCIPAL

12.1 Right to access information. You may obtain a summary of the personal data we process
about you, the processing activities we carry out, and the identities of all other Data
Fiduciaries and Data Processors with whom we have shared it, along with a description of the
data shared. Request this from Settings > Privacy > Download My Data. We respond within 15
working days.

12.2 Right to correction, completion and updating. You may request correction of inaccurate
or misleading personal data, completion of incomplete data, and updating of data that has
changed. Most fields can be edited directly in your profile; for KYC-locked fields, raise a
request and we will act within 15 working days.

12.3 Right to erasure. You may request erasure of your personal data. We will erase it unless
retention is necessary for the specified purpose or for compliance with a law in force, in
which case we will tell you precisely which law requires retention and for how long.

12.4 Right of nomination. You may nominate any other individual to exercise your rights under
the Act on your behalf in the event of your death or of your incapacity by reason of unsoundness
of mind or infirmity of body. Register or change your nominee at Settings > Privacy > Nominee.

12.5 Right to grievance redressal. You have the right to a readily available means of
registering a grievance with us. See Section 13.

14. CHILDREN'S AND PERSONS WITH DISABILITY DATA

PayGrid's services are available only to individuals aged 18 years and above. Where we do
process the personal data of a child, or of a person with disability who has a lawful
guardian, we obtain verifiable consent from the parent or lawful guardian before any
processing begins, using a government-issued identity token through a DigiLocker-based
verification flow.

We do not undertake any tracking or behavioural monitoring of children, and we do not serve
targeted advertising directed at children. We do not undertake any processing of a child's
personal data that is likely to cause any detrimental effect on the well-being of the child.

15. SIGNIFICANT DATA FIDUCIARY OBLIGATIONS

PayGrid has been notified as a Significant Data Fiduciary having regard to the volume and
sensitivity of the personal data we process. Accordingly we have:

Appointed a Data Protection Officer based in India who reports to our Board of Directors.
Appointed an independent Data Auditor to carry out an annual Data Protection Impact
Assessment and a periodic compliance audit.
Instituted algorithmic due-diligence review for every model used to process personal data.
Adopted additional measures prescribed for Significant Data Fiduciaries by the Central
Government.

16. YOUR DUTIES AS A DATA PRINCIPAL

Under Section 15 of the Act you are required to comply with applicable law when exercising
your rights, not to impersonate another person when providing your personal data, not to
suppress any material information when providing personal data for any document or identity
proof, not to register a false or frivolous grievance or complaint, and to furnish only such
information as is verifiably authentic when exercising your right to correction or erasure.
