# LearnLoop Education Technologies Private Limited — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 November 2025_

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how LearnLoop Education Technologies Private Limited
("LearnLoop", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores and shares your personal data when you use
our learning platform, mobile application and related services.

LearnLoop is the Data Fiduciary responsible for your personal data.

**Registered office:** 2nd Floor, Cyber Heights, Hitec City, Hyderabad, Telangana 500081
**Email:** support@learnloop.in

## 2. Personal data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

- **Account information** — learner name, email address, mobile number, password
- **Profile information** — school or college, grade or year, subjects of interest
- **Learning data** — courses enrolled, lesson progress, quiz scores, time spent
- **Device information** — device identifier, IP address, operating system, app version
- **Payment information** — billing name, address and payment instrument reference

## 3. Why we process your personal data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

- To create and operate your learner account
- To deliver course content and track your progress
- To issue certificates and transcripts
- To process subscription payments
- To provide learner support
- To send service updates, and where you have opted in, course recommendations

We do not use your learning data for any purpose other than those listed above without
giving you a fresh notice.

## 4. Consent

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which we obtain through an
unticked checkbox shown alongside this notice at the time of registration. Consent for
promotional emails is sought separately, and declining it does not restrict your access to
any course.

You may withdraw your consent by emailing support@learnloop.in. We will action withdrawal
requests within a reasonable period. Please note that some processing may continue where we
have a legal obligation to retain records.

## 5. Sharing your personal data

We share personal data with:

- **Payment gateways** — to process subscription payments
- **Cloud hosting providers** — to host the platform and course content
- **Email and SMS providers** — to deliver notifications
- **Analytics providers** — to understand how learners use the platform

Each provider is engaged under a written contract requiring them to process personal data
only on our instructions and to maintain appropriate security measures. We do not sell
personal data.

## 6. Storage and transfer

Personal data is stored on servers located in India. Some analytics and email processing
occurs on infrastructure located in the United States and the European Union under
contractual safeguards.

## 7. Retention

We retain learner account data for as long as the account remains active. Certificates and
academic transcripts are retained indefinitely so that learners can re-download them.
Payment records are retained for seven years in line with tax law.

## 8. Security

We protect your personal data using TLS encryption in transit, encryption at rest for
payment references, role-based access controls, and regular security reviews. Access to
production systems requires multi-factor authentication.

## 9. Your rights

As a Data Principal you may:

- **Access** a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we process it
- **Correct** any personal data that is inaccurate or incomplete
- **Erase** your personal data, subject to our retention obligations

To exercise these rights, write to support@learnloop.in from your registered email address.
We aim to respond within 30 days.

## 10. Grievances

If you have a complaint about how we handle your personal data, please contact our support
team at support@learnloop.in with the subject line "Privacy Grievance". We will acknowledge
your complaint and work to resolve it.

## 11. Learners under 18

A large proportion of our learners are school students. Where a learner indicates that they
are under 18, we ask for a parent or guardian email address at the time of registration and
send a confirmation email to that address before the account is activated.

We do not show third-party advertising anywhere on the LearnLoop platform.

## 12. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and measure platform usage.
You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised policy will be posted on
this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
